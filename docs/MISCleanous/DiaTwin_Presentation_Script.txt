SLIDE 3 — WHAT IS DIATWIN?
A pharmacodynamic digital twin for T1D
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DiaTwin builds a real-time model of each patient's glucose state — tracking how
insulin and carbohydrates behave physiologically — to answer three critical
questions every minute:

  - What is my BG right now?
  - Where will it go in the next few hours?
  - Is it safe to eat or dose?

[Core values]

IOB — Insulin On Board
Insulin still pharmacologically active — calculated via trapezoidal integration

MOB — Meal On Board
Carbohydrates still being absorbed — modelled via gamma-distribution kinetics

Net — Net BG Effect
Meal impact minus insulin impact — positive = rising, negative = falling

SLIDE 4 — THE ENGINE
Pharmacodynamics Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Meal Absorption (MOB)
Gamma-distribution curve modelling:
  - Delayed gastric emptying onset
  - Peak absorption rise
  - Gradual metabolic tail
  - 5 absorption speeds: very slow to very fast
  - Personalised absorption modifier per patient

Insulin Activity (IOB)
Two curve families — S3 Clinical Guidelines:

  Peaked Insulins (rapid, short, intermediate acting)
  Gamma distribution with type-specific parameters

  Peakless Insulins (long-acting, ultra-long acting)
  4-phase model, capped at 75% max activity

The same mathematical model runs identically in Python (backend) and TypeScript
(frontend) — guaranteeing consistency across all platforms.

SLIDE 5 — FOR PATIENTS
Predictive Self-Care
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Real-Time IOB & MOB
See exactly how much insulin and meal carbs are still active — eliminating
guesswork before a correction dose.

Advisory Bolus Calculator
Dose suggestions accounting for current BG, active IOB, planned meal, activity
level, and time-of-day resistance.

Pre-Bolus Timing
Recommends optimal pre-meal injection window (0–20 min) based on the glycaemic
index of the logged meal.

Structured Meal Logging
Extensive food database including local cuisine categories — reducing
carb-counting burden and improving adherence.

BG Rise Prediction
MOB is converted into an estimated future glucose rise — letting patients
anticipate hyperglycaemia before it occurs.

Activity Adjustment
Physical activity logging automatically adjusts dose suggestions — supporting
safer exercise management.

SLIDE 6 — FOR PHYSICIANS
Evidence-Based Remote Monitoring
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Remote Patient Monitoring
Authorised physicians access a dedicated dashboard: BG history, meal logs,
insulin records, and activity data for informed pre-consultation review.

Glycaemic Response Analysis
Meal-glucose correlation charts reveal postprandial response patterns across
days or weeks — objective evidence for ICR or ISF titration.

Clinical Parameter Review
All patient-specific constants (ICR, CF, target glucose, absorption modifiers)
are visible in a structured view.

Pattern-Based Titration
Time-in-range statistics, SMBG analysis, and glycaemic variability metrics
(MAGE, CV%) provide a structured evidence base for dose conversations.

Consent-Gated Access
Bilateral authorisation model ensures patient data sovereignty. Physicians see
only consented patients — GDPR-aligned clinical data governance.

Multi-Patient Overview
Full panel of authorised patients, each searchable by name — enabling efficient
review workflows in specialist clinics or GP settings.

At-a-Glance Metabolic Control
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ActiveEffectsDisplay
Primary home-screen widget — refreshes every 60 seconds synced to wall-clock
:00 boundary.

Metrics displayed:
  - Last Meter Reading: Most recent fingerstick with time-since badge (e.g. "14 min ago")
  - Insulin Card (IOB): BG reduction already absorbed / further drop still to come
  - Meal Card (MOB): BG rise already absorbed / hidden rise still coming
  - Current Est. BG: Baseline + cumulative net — colour-coded green/orange/red
  - Projected Final BG: Where BG will land if nothing new is added

Additional Visualizations:
  - EffectsVisualization: IOB + MOB evolution over next few hours
  - BloodSugarVisualization: Full BG history vs. model estimates
  - MealVisualization: Active meals, absorption rates, pending BG rise

SLIDE 13 — PHYSICIAN CONTROL
All Parameters Set & Managed by Doctors
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DiaTwin does not allow patients to modify clinical parameters. Every threshold,
ratio, and limit is prescribed and adjusted exclusively by the treating
physician — ensuring safety, clinical accountability, and regulatory compliance.

Parameters (all Physician only):

Insulin-to-Carb Ratio (ICR)  [Physician only]
Defines how many grams of carbohydrate one unit of insulin covers. Personalised
per patient and time-of-day.

Correction / Sensitivity Factor  [Physician only]
How much 1 unit of insulin lowers blood glucose. Used to calculate correction
boluses against a target BG.

Target Blood Glucose  [Physician only]
The individual BG goal for each patient — used to calculate the correction delta
and colour-code estimates.

Safety Limits & Dose Caps  [Physician only]
Maximum allowed bolus per dose and per day. Hard stops prevent the system from
suggesting unsafe corrections.

Absorption Modifiers  [Physician only]
Patient-specific multiplier that scales the gamma-distribution absorption curves
for meals and insulin activity.

Basal Rate Equivalent  [Physician only]
Long-acting insulin baseline parameters — onset, duration, and activity profile
used in IOB calculations.

Patients interact only with logging and viewing functions. Parameter access is
gated behind physician authentication.
